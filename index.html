    If something sounds wrong on iPhone
    • No sound at all: raise the volume with the side buttons, and check that the earbuds are connected.
    • The sound does not move, or both ears hear the same thing: in Settings, open Accessibility, then Audio & Visual, and turn Mono Audio off.
    • The music stops when the phone locks: choose Go dark above. The page turns black and the phone stays awake, so the music carries on.
    • The music is silent after you come back from another app: press Resume, or Stop and then Begin.
